When you delete your account, your personal data is permanently removed. This cannot be undone.

## How to delete

1. **In the app (recommended):** Profile → Delete Account. You need to confirm your identity; deletion happens immediately.
2. **By email:** send an email with the subject **Account Deletion Request** to support@alpcakin.com from the email address registered to your account. Your request is processed within 30 days at the latest.

## What is deleted

- Account details (email, name, @tag, profile photo)
- IBANs
- Friendships, blocks and pending invites
- Group memberships (groups with no remaining members are deleted with all their records)
- Offline contacts, their records and receipt photos
- Notification and app preferences, consent records
- Push notification token, device language and time zone; if you used Sign in with Apple, the app's access on Apple's side

## Shared records

Expense and debt records shared with other users are **anonymised**, not fully deleted, when your account is deleted. Your name appears as "Deleted User" in these records so other users keep their debt history. Receipt photos attached to shared expenses stay with those records and are deleted when the expense is deleted.

You cannot delete your account while you have an open debt with another user; you will be asked to settle the balance first. If you cannot settle it, contact us by email.

> Deletion cannot be undone. A deleted account cannot be recovered.

## Retention after deletion

Personal data is removed from all systems within **30 days** of a deletion request at the latest. Anonymised shared records (see above) may be kept permanently.

## Contact

For all data requests under the GDPR or the KVKK (access, correction, erasure, objection): support@alpcakin.com
