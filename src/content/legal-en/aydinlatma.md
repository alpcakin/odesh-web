## 1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is **Alp Çakın**, a private individual based in Hungary (European Union) who develops and operates the odesh app. You can reach the controller at **support@alpcakin.com** for any privacy question or request (see section 9).

Because the controller is established in the EU, the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") applies to all users, wherever they live. For users in Türkiye, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") also applies; the Turkish Privacy Notice (Aydınlatma Metni) has the same content.

IBANs and financial records are not special categories of data under GDPR Art. 9, but because they are sensitive they are protected with additional technical and organisational measures. We never ask for or process special categories of data (such as health, religion or biometric data).

Providing your name and email address is necessary to create an account; without them we cannot provide the service. Everything else (IBAN, photos, receipts, usage data) is optional.

### 2.1 Sign in with Apple and Google

Instead of an email and password you can choose "Continue with Apple" or "Continue with Google". In that case:

- The provider sends us only your **name**, **email address** and, if your provider account has one, a **profile photo link**. The photo link is stored with your sign-in record but not used in the app. We do not access contacts, calendars, photos or any other data.
- Your password never reaches us; you authenticate on the provider's own screen.
- If you use Apple's **"Hide My Email"**, we only receive the relay address Apple creates, not your real email address.
- When you delete your account, the app's access is also revoked on Apple's side if you used Sign in with Apple.

Social sign-in is optional; you can always register with an email address and password.

### 2.2 Data about people who do not use the app (offline contacts and members)

The "offline contact" and "offline member" features let you track debts with people who do not use the app (a landlord, a shop, a friend). These records contain only the **name** and the **optional IBAN** that you enter.

- We store this data on the basis of your legitimate interest in keeping track of your own debts, and only on your instructions. You are responsible for entering it lawfully (with the person's knowledge and for a real debt; Terms of Use section 3). Records you keep purely for your own personal purposes may fall under the household exemption of GDPR Art. 2(2)(c) for you; this does not remove our own security obligations.
- Offline contact records are visible **only to you**; offline member records are visible **only to the members of that group**. They are never shown to other users or shared with any third party for advertising. Usage analytics only count that "a record was created"; the person's name, IBAN and amounts are never sent.
- When you delete the contact, the IBAN is deleted; the debt history is kept only as your own record together with the name. When you delete your account, all your offline contacts, their records and receipts are deleted.
- The person named in such a record can ask us at support@alpcakin.com to delete it; we forward the request to the user who created it or delete the record directly.

### 2.3 Data other users enter about you

Your friends and group members can create expense and payment records that name you as a party. We receive these records from the person who creates them, and you can see them in the app. Only people who are your friends or share a group with you can add you to a record; someone you have blocked cannot.

### 2.4 Device permissions

- **Notifications:** only if you allow them. We ask the first time it is useful, not during sign-up.
- **Camera / photo library:** only when you choose to add a receipt or profile photo. Your library is not scanned.
- **Contacts, location, microphone:** never accessed. Friends are added only by @tag or invite link.

### 2.5 Usage analytics require your consent

Usage analytics are **off until you agree**. New users are asked with an optional, unticked box during profile setup; existing users are asked once in the app. Nothing is sent before you agree, including from the screens before sign-in. You can withdraw your consent at any time in **Settings > Privacy & Data > Share Usage Data**; withdrawing does not affect processing that took place before. Declining has no effect on the rest of the app.

## 3. Who receives your data

### 3.1 Other users

By its nature, the app shows some of your data to other users:

| Data | Visible to |
|---|---|
| Name, @tag, profile photo | Your friends, members of groups you share, someone who sent you a friend request, people who share a record with you. Anyone who knows your exact @tag can find you |
| IBAN | Only your friends and members of groups you share; nobody if "Share My IBAN with Friends" is off |
| Expenses, payments and receipts | Only the parties to that record; for group records, all group members |

A person you have blocked cannot find you, send you requests or see your profile.

### 3.3 Authorities

If a competent authority (such as a court, prosecutor or data protection authority) makes a lawful request, we may disclose data limited to the scope of that request.

### 3.4 Transfers outside the EU

Your data is hosted by Supabase in **Ireland (EU)**; crash reports and usage analytics are also processed in the EU. Expo and Resend, used for notification delivery and email, are in the USA. These transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (GDPR Art. 46(2)(c)), which are part of the providers' terms; Resend is also certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (GDPR Art. 45). You can ask for a copy of the safeguards at the address in section 9.

## 4. Where your data comes from

Your data is collected electronically:

- directly from you: sign-up form, profile and settings screens, expense/debt/payment screens, photo selection;
- from other users: records that name you as a party (section 2.3);
- from sign-in providers: only if you use that option (section 2.1);
- from your device and your use of the app: push token, device language and time zone, device and operating system details, crash reports, usage events (only with consent), IP address.

## 5. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account, profile, preferences, relationships, IBAN | Until you delete your account |
| Expense, debt and payment records | Until you delete your account; after that, your fields are anonymised (see below) |
| Receipt photos | Until the record or the account is deleted |
| Push token, device language and time zone | At most 90 days after the device was last used; immediately when the account is deleted |
| Notification delivery logs | 90 days |
| Authentication and security logs | For the infrastructure provider's log retention period, at most 12 months |
| Crash reports (Sentry) | 90 days |
| Usage analytics (PostHog) | At most 12 months; after account deletion the pseudonymous ID can no longer be linked to any account |
| Consent records | Until you delete your account |

When you delete your account, your personal data is deleted or anonymised. In expense and payment records that also concern other users, your fields are anonymised as "Deleted User"; the rest of the record is kept so the other party keeps their debt history. Details: "Account and Data Deletion".

## 6. Automated decisions and profiling

No decision that has legal effects on you or similarly significantly affects you is made by automated means (GDPR Art. 22). Debt reminders are scheduled automatically; this never changes a record or its amount, and the person who owes can turn reminders off completely.

## 7. Children

odesh is meant for people aged **16 or over**. We do not knowingly process data of anyone under 16; if we learn of it, the account and related data are deleted.

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** your data and get a copy of it (Art. 15);
- have inaccurate data **corrected** (Art. 16);
- have your data **erased** (Art. 17);
- **restrict** processing (Art. 18);
- receive the data you gave us in a portable format (**data portability**, Art. 20);
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21);
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting earlier processing (Art. 7(3));
- **lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work, or with the Hungarian authority (Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság, naih.hu).

Users in Türkiye also have the rights in Article 11 of the KVKK and can complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.

In the app you can always correct your profile and IBAN, turn usage data off in Settings, and delete your account from the Profile screen.

## 9. How to make a request

Email **support@alpcakin.com** from the email address registered to your account, with your name and what you are asking for. Requests are free and answered within **one month**; for complex requests this can be extended by two further months, in which case we tell you within the first month.

## 10. Changes

We update this policy when our processing changes. The current version is always available in the app (Settings > Legal) and on this page. When the version changes, the app asks you to review and accept it the next time you open it.
